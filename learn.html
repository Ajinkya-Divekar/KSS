How many PDF guides are there, and how long are they?

There are several PDF guides on the tissue paper business, and the page counts vary. The Starter Guide is 80 pages and covers the industry overview, products, machinery, manufacturing processes, raw materials, sourcing and business setup fundamentals. Ask on WhatsApp which guide suits you.

Who is this for?

Aspiring entrepreneurs exploring the tissue paper business, people planning to enter the industry, and entrepreneurs or investors who want practical exposure.

How do the Zoom meetings work?

They are one-to-one Zoom sessions with me, covering business setup guidance, machinery and raw material insights, and relevant industry contacts. We schedule them on WhatsApp after you enquire.

What happens on a plant visit, and which cities do you cover?

I visit the plant and give practical, on-site guidance. Visits are available across India, and applicable travel expenses are communicated before booking.
